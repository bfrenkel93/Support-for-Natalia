When someone you love is grieving

Everyone asks the
same question.

“What can I do?”

There are meals to bring and errands to run. Kids who still need joy and normalcy. Stories worth keeping. And people who want to show up but don’t always know how.

Family Grief Support gives a grieving family one private page where the people who love them can coordinate meals, help with the kids, practical needs, memories, and the months that come after.

Built for the long after

Grief keeps going.
So should support.

The first weeks bring meals. Flowers. Messages. People checking in.

Then the messages slow, and everyone returns to their routines.

The grieving family doesn’t.

A birthday. An anniversary. The first day of school. The holidays. A hard week. A child’s milestone. An ordinary Tuesday, months later.

Because showing up shouldn’t have an expiration date.

The family shares what’s ahead

They can add the moments still to come: a birthday, a school game, a hard anniversary, or a week that needs a little more help.

Everyone can stay close

Friends and family can choose to keep showing up when those moments come, months after the first few weeks have passed.

Made with grief in mind

There is no perfect thing to say.

Sometimes the most meaningful thing is simply to show up.

  • No performative condolences.
  • No pressure to respond.
  • No expectation that grief follows a timeline.

Just a simple way for people to care for one another when life becomes impossibly hard.

We can’t take away someone’s grief.

But we can make sure they don’t have to carry everything else alone.
